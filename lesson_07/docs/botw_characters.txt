Link is the silent protagonist of Breath of the Wild. He was the appointed knight of Princess Zelda and wielder of the Master Sword. One hundred years before the events of the game, he was gravely wounded in the battle against Calamity Ganon and placed in the Shrine of Resurrection to heal. When he wakes up, he has lost his memories, which the player gradually recovers by finding twelve memory locations across Hyrule.

Princess Zelda is the princess of Hyrule and a scholar of ancient Sheikah technology. She struggled for years to awaken the sacred power inherited from her mother, which made her feel inadequate next to Link. During the Great Calamity she held Calamity Ganon back inside Hyrule Castle for one hundred years, using her sealing power. Her voice guides Link throughout the game.

Calamity Ganon is the main antagonist, a primordial embodiment of hatred and malice. It was sealed away in the past, but returned and possessed the ancient Guardians and the four Divine Beasts. The final battle takes place inside Hyrule Castle.

The four Champions were chosen to pilot the Divine Beasts. Mipha, princess of the Zora, piloted Vah Ruta. Daruk, leader of the Gorons, piloted Vah Rudania. Revali, a proud Rito warrior, piloted Vah Medoh. Urbosa, chief of the Gerudo, piloted Vah Naboris. Each Champion died when Calamity Ganon took control of their Divine Beast, and each leaves behind a special ability, such as Mipha's Grace or Daruk's Protection, which Link receives after freeing the Beast.

Other notable characters include King Rhoam, Zelda's father, who appears as a ghost, and Impa, the elder of Kakariko Village. Kass is a traveling Rito musician who shares clues about shrines.
