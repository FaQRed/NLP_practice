The Divine Beasts are four giant ancient machines built by the Sheikah to fight Calamity Ganon. Each one is shaped like an animal and is a dungeon in the game. Vah Ruta is an elephant located in Lake Ruto near Zora's Domain. Vah Rudania is a salamander on Death Mountain. Vah Medoh is a bird flying above Rito Village. Vah Naboris is a camel that walks across the Gerudo Desert during sandstorms.

Each Divine Beast has the same structure. Link enters it, uses the Sheikah Slate to activate five terminals, and then reaches the main control unit, where he faces a Blight Ganon boss. For example, Waterblight Ganon is the boss of Vah Ruta, Fireblight Ganon is in Vah Rudania, Windblight Ganon is in Vah Medoh, and Thunderblight Ganon is in Vah Naboris. After the boss is defeated, the Champion's spirit is freed and gives Link a special power. Completing the Divine Beasts is optional but makes the final fight easier.

Shrines are small puzzle rooms with one challenge each, found across the map. There are one hundred and twenty shrines in the base game. Finishing a shrine gives a Spirit Orb. Four Spirit Orbs can be traded at a Goddess Statue for either a Heart Container, which gives one more heart, or a Stamina Vessel, which gives more stamina. Some shrines test combat skills, others involve physics puzzles with balls, levers and moving platforms, and others are hidden behind riddles or side quests.

Korok seeds are another collectible. There are 900 hidden Korok seeds. They are given to Hestu, who uses them to expand the weapon, bow and shield inventory.
