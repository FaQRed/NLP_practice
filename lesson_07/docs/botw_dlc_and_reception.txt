Breath of the Wild received two downloadable content packs as part of the Expansion Pass. The first, The Master Trials, was released in June 2017. It added the Trial of the Sword challenge, a Hero's Path mode that shows the player's route on the map, and a Master Mode, a harder difficulty in which enemies regenerate health and get stronger.

The second pack, The Champions' Ballad, was released in December 2017. It adds a new set of shrines and a quest that tells the story of the four Champions, the hundred years before the Great Calamity, and finally gives Link the Master Cycle Zero, a motorcycle he can summon.

The game was a commercial and critical success. It sold over ten million copies on the Nintendo Switch within a few years and more than thirty million in total. Critics praised its open world, its sense of discovery, its physics engine, its art style, and the freedom given to the player. It won The Game Awards 2017 Game of the Year. It also influenced many later open-world games, with developers describing its design as a new standard.

The sequel, The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom, was released on May 12, 2023. It takes place in the same Hyrule and adds sky islands, a Depths underground region, and abilities such as Ultrahand and Fuse. The soundtrack of Breath of the Wild is minimalist, with gentle piano pieces that play during exploration, and was composed by Manaka Tataishi, Yasuaki Iwata and Hajime Wakai.
