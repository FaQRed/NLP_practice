Gameplay in Breath of the Wild is built around freedom and experimentation. After leaving the Great Plateau, the player can go anywhere. Link can climb mountains and trees, swim, and glide through the air with the paraglider. Climbing and gliding use up the stamina wheel, which can be extended by collecting Heart Containers or Stamina Vessels from shrines.

Weapons, bows and shields have durability and break after a certain amount of use. The player constantly has to find, pick up and switch between swords, spears, clubs and shields. The Master Sword is special: it does not break permanently, but runs out of energy and recharges after some time. It is found in the Korok Forest, but pulling it from the pedestal requires at least thirteen hearts.

Cooking is an important mechanic. Link can collect ingredients such as apples, mushrooms, herbs, fish and monster parts, and cook them over a fire in a pot. Meals restore hearts, and special elixirs give temporary effects like cold resistance, heat resistance, extra speed or stealth. Combining ingredients with the same effect makes the effect stronger.

Weather and temperature affect the player. In cold areas Link needs warm clothes or spicy food, and in the desert or near volcanoes he needs fire-resistant gear. Thunderstorms attract lightning to metal equipment, so the player should drop metal weapons when it rains heavily. Wearing armor sets and upgrading them with the Great Fairies gives extra defense and bonuses.

The game also has a day and night cycle. At night more dangerous enemies appear, and Blood Moon rises from time to time, resurrecting all defeated monsters.
