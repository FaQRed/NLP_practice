The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild is an action-adventure game developed and published by Nintendo. It was released on March 3, 2017 for the Nintendo Switch and the Wii U. The game was directed by Hidemaro Fujibayashi, produced by Eiji Aonuma, and Hajime Wakai served as art director. Breath of the Wild is the nineteenth main entry in The Legend of Zelda series and was a launch title for the Nintendo Switch.

The game is set in the kingdom of Hyrule. The player controls Link, who wakes up after a hundred years of sleep in the Shrine of Resurrection with no memory of his past. A mysterious voice guides him out of the shrine onto the Great Plateau. From there the player is free to explore the whole world in any order. The main goal is to defeat Calamity Ganon, a malevolent force that has taken over Hyrule Castle and corrupted the Divine Beasts and the ancient Guardians.

Breath of the Wild broke with many conventions of the series. Instead of a linear sequence of dungeons, it offers an open world that can be explored from the very beginning. The player can climb almost any surface, glide with a paraglider, cook food, and use a physics and chemistry engine that lets fire, wind, electricity and metal interact with the environment. The game received universal acclaim and won many Game of the Year awards in 2017. It is often listed among the greatest video games of all time. A sequel, The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom, was released in May 2023.
