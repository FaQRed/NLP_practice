The Sheikah Slate is a tablet-like device made by the ancient Sheikah. Link receives it at the beginning of the game from the Shrine of Resurrection. It serves as a map, a camera, an encyclopedia called the Hyrule Compendium, and as the source of Link's special abilities, called runes. Additional runes are unlocked in the first shrines on the Great Plateau after meeting the Old Man.

The Remote Bombs rune creates round and cube bombs that can be detonated at any time. They are used to destroy rocks, break walls and damage enemies. Magnesis lets Link move metallic objects with a magnet, for example to lift chests from water or build bridges from metal blocks. Stasis freezes an object in time. Link can then hit it several times, and all the accumulated force is released at once when the effect ends. Cryonis creates pillars of ice out of water, which can be used as platforms, bridges or elevators.

Other features of the Slate include the Sheikah Sensor, which detects nearby shrines and other targets, the camera for taking pictures of items and monsters, and the ability to place map stamps and pins. Later, the player can use the Slate with towers: climbing a Sheikah Tower unlocks the map of the nearby region. There are fifteen towers across Hyrule.

Amiibo figures can also be scanned with the Slate to receive rewards or summon special items.
