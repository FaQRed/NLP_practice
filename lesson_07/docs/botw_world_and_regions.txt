The world of Breath of the Wild is divided into several large regions. The game begins on the Great Plateau, an isolated tutorial area surrounded by cliffs. It contains four shrines and the Temple of Time. Once the player gets the paraglider from the Old Man, they can leave the plateau and glide down into the rest of Hyrule.

Hyrule Field is the central plain, with Hyrule Castle in the middle, where Calamity Ganon waits. Kakariko Village is a hidden Sheikah village in the mountains where Impa lives. Hateno Village in the east has a laboratory and a house that the player can buy.

The four main regions are connected to the four Divine Beasts. The Zora's Domain in the east is a waterfall city where Vah Ruta waits, a giant elephant. Death Mountain in the north is home to the Gorons and Vah Rudania, a salamander. Rito Village in the northwest, in the Hebra mountains, is the home of the Rito and Vah Medoh, a giant bird. The Gerudo Desert in the west is home to the Gerudo, who are all women, and Vah Naboris, a camel. Gerudo Town can only be entered by men in a disguise.

The map also includes the Korok Forest, with the Great Deku Tree, and Lanayru and Faron, wet and tropical regions. Across Hyrule the player can find 120 shrines, 900 Korok seeds, and stables where they can rest and keep their horses. Horses can be tamed in the wild and registered at a stable.

Each region has its own climate and music, and nearly every part of the map can be reached from the start, so the player can fight Ganon immediately if they want.
